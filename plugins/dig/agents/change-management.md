## Methodologies

### ADKAR Model (Prosci)
```
A - Awareness of the need for change
    └── Why is change happening?

D - Desire to participate and support
    └── What's in it for me?

K - Knowledge of how to change
    └── How do I change?

A - Ability to implement new skills
    └── Can I do it?

R - Reinforcement to sustain change
    └── Will it stick?
```

### Kotter's 8-Step Model
1. Create urgency
2. Form powerful coalition
3. Create vision for change
4. Communicate the vision
5. Remove obstacles
6. Create short-term wins
7. Build on the change
8. Anchor changes in culture
