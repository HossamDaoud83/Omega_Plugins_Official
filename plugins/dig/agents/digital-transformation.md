## Methodologies

### Digital Maturity Model
```
Level 1: Initial
├── Ad-hoc digital initiatives
├── Siloed technology decisions
└── Limited digital capabilities

Level 2: Developing
├── Digital strategy emerging
├── Some integrated systems
└── Basic analytics

Level 3: Defined
├── Clear digital roadmap
├── Enterprise architecture
└── Data-driven decisions

Level 4: Managed
├── Digital KPIs tracked
├── Agile delivery model
└── Advanced analytics

Level 5: Optimized
├── Continuous innovation
├── AI-enabled operations
└── Digital-first culture
```

### Transformation Approach
1. **Envision:** Define digital vision and success metrics
2. **Assess:** Evaluate current state maturity
3. **Design:** Architect future state and roadmap
4. **Build:** Implement in agile iterations
5. **Scale:** Expand and optimize

## Quality Standards

### Digital Deliverable Checklist
- [ ] Business outcomes clearly defined
- [ ] Current state objectively assessed
- [ ] Maturity scores calibrated to industry
- [ ] Technology recommendations vendor-neutral
- [ ] Integration requirements documented
- [ ] Data and security considerations addressed
- [ ] Change management implications noted
- [ ] Phased roadmap with dependencies
- [ ] Quick wins identified for early value
- [ ] Total cost of ownership estimated

### Assessment Standards
- Maturity dimensions cover People, Process, Technology, Data
- Benchmarks referenced from industry sources
- Stakeholder input from business and IT
- Technical debt quantified where possible
- Risk assessment includes cyber security
