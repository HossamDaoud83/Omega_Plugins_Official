# Financial Services Regulatory Framework

## US Banking Regulators

### Federal Regulators
| Regulator | Jurisdiction |
|-----------|--------------|
| OCC | National banks, federal savings associations |
| Federal Reserve | Bank holding companies, state member banks |
| FDIC | State non-member banks, deposit insurance |
| CFPB | Consumer financial products (>$10B assets) |
| NCUA | Credit unions |

### Examination Cycle
- Large banks: Continuous examination
- Community banks: 12-18 month cycle
- Risk-focused approach

### Examination Findings
| Finding | Severity | Response |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Observation | Low | Track for improvement |
| MRA | Medium | Formal response required |
| MRIA | High | Immediate action required |
| Consent Order | Critical | Formal enforcement |

## Key Banking Regulations

### BSA/AML (Bank Secrecy Act / Anti-Money Laundering)
- Customer Identification Program (CIP)
- Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
- Beneficial Ownership
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs)
- Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs)
- OFAC screening

### Consumer Protection
- Truth in Lending (Regulation Z)
- Truth in Savings (Regulation DD)
- Equal Credit Opportunity (Regulation B)
- Fair Credit Reporting Act
- Fair Debt Collection Practices Act
- UDAAP (Unfair, Deceptive, Abusive Acts or Practices)

### Capital Requirements (Basel III/IV)
- Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1): 4.5%
- Tier 1 Capital: 6%
- Total Capital: 8%
- Capital Conservation Buffer: 2.5%
- Countercyclical Buffer: 0-2.5%
- G-SIB Surcharge: 1-3.5%

## Insurance Regulation

### State-Based System
- Each state has insurance commissioner
- NAIC model laws provide consistency
- Rate regulation varies by state
- Market conduct examinations

### Key Requirements
- Solvency requirements
- Reserve requirements
- Policy form approval
- Rate filing
- Producer licensing

---

## Model Risk Management (SR 11-7)

### Key Requirements
- Model development documentation
- Independent model validation
- Ongoing monitoring
- Model inventory
- Governance framework
- Board and senior management oversight

### Model Risk Assessment
| Factor | Considerations |
|--------|----------------|
| Materiality | Financial impact, decision importance |
| Complexity | Model sophistication |
| Uncertainty | Data quality, assumptions |

---

## Operational Resilience

### Key Components
- Business impact analysis
- Recovery time objectives
- Third-party risk management
- Cyber resilience
- Business continuity planning
- Incident management

### Sound Practices (SR 20-24)
- Identify critical operations
- Set tolerances for disruption
- Test ability to operate through disruption
- Remediate weaknesses
- Board and senior management oversight
