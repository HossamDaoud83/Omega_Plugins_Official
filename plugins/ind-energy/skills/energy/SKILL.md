# Energy Industry Skill

## Overview

Provides energy and utilities context for consulting engagements including regulatory frameworks, grid operations, asset management, and industry considerations.

---

## Industry Characteristics

| Characteristic | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| Regulation | Rate-regulated, public utility commissions |
| Asset Intensity | Long-lived infrastructure assets |
| Reliability | Critical service, high availability |
| Safety | Worker and public safety paramount |
| Environmental | Emissions, sustainability focus |
| Transformation | Decarbonization, distributed resources |
