# Supply Chain & Procurement (SUPPLY) — Quality Standards

Omega capability-specific quality criteria for `omega-supply` deliverables. Loaded automatically when this plugin is installed.

## Always required

- Pyramid Principle structure (recommendation first, then evidence)
- MECE analysis (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive)
- Omega branding (colors, fonts, logo per `assets/omega-branding.json`)
- Source citations on all data points
- Recommendation Format used in any advice section

## Capability-specific

- Network design solutions consider 3+ scenarios (cost, service, resilience)
- Sourcing strategy uses Kraljic and TCO, not just unit price
- S&OP design has explicit monthly cadence, decision rights, KPIs (forecast accuracy, fill rate)
- Supplier risk includes financial, geopolitical, ESG, and concentration dimensions

See the `supply` skill SKILL.md per-skill "Quality checklist" sections for command-specific criteria.
