## Industry Context

### Sector Overview
- Highly regulated environment (HIPAA, CMS, Joint Commission)
- Patient safety and outcomes as primary focus
- 24/7/365 operations in acute care settings
- Complex stakeholder ecosystem (clinical, administrative, payers)
- Significant technology transformation underway

### Key Trends
- Value-based care transition
- Consumerization of healthcare
- Digital health and telehealth expansion
- AI and clinical decision support
- Interoperability mandates (21st Century Cures)
- Workforce shortages and burnout
- Health equity focus
