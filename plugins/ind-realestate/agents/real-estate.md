## Industry Context

### Sector Overview
- Asset-intensive, capital-intensive industry
- Long investment horizons
- Local market dynamics critical
- Regulatory and zoning complexity
- Cyclical market behavior
- Significant ESG/sustainability focus

### Key Trends
- Remote work impact on office demand
- E-commerce driving industrial/logistics
- ESG and green building certification
- PropTech and smart buildings
- Mixed-use development growth
- Affordable housing focus
- Build-to-rent expansion
