# Workshop Facilitation

## When to use
Triggered when the consultant needs to plan a discovery workshop, alignment session, decision workshop, or design sprint with the client.

## Workshop archetypes

| Archetype | Purpose | Duration | Typical attendance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Surface current state, pain points, ambitions | 2-4 hrs | 8-15 stakeholders |
| Alignment | Align on objectives, scope, success criteria | 2 hrs | Sponsor + 5-8 leaders |
| Prioritization | Sequence initiatives by impact / effort | 3 hrs | Decision-makers |
| Design sprint | Co-design future state | 1-3 days | 6-10 mixed roles |
| Decision workshop | Drive a binary decision with full context | 90 min | Decision-maker + advisors |

## Standard agenda template (3-hour workshop)

1. **Welcome + intent (10 min).** Sponsor opens. State purpose, success criteria, decision rights.
2. **Context (20 min).** Pre-read recap, framing of the problem, key data.
3. **Working block 1 (60 min).** Structured exercise (see Exercises below).
4. **Break (15 min).**
5. **Working block 2 (45 min).** Synthesis or second exercise.
6. **Decisions + next steps (20 min).** Crisp decisions, owners, dates. No "TBD".
7. **Wrap (10 min).** Sponsor closes. Capture immediate sentiment.

## Exercises by purpose

| Goal | Exercise | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Surface ambitions | "Headlines from 2027" | List of headline statements |
| Surface pain points | "If only we could…" + dot vote | Ranked pain points |
| Prioritize | 2x2 impact/effort | Quadrant placement of initiatives |
| Decide | Disagree-and-commit register | Documented dissent + decision |
| Align scope | In-scope / out-of-scope T-chart | Scope statement |
| Generate options | "How might we…" + brainwriting | List of candidate solutions |

## Energizers (use sparingly)
- Two truths + one lie (5 min, builds rapport)
- One-word check-in (2 min, gauges room temperature)
- Stand up if… (2 min, surfaces commonality)

## Output capture rules

- One scribe with explicit role; do NOT split capture across the room
- Capture decisions, owners, dates immediately on a visible surface
- Photograph all flip charts BEFORE leaving
- Post-workshop synthesis lands in the engagement brain within 24 hours, not "soon"
- Action items go straight into the engagement `raid.json` or tracker

## Quality bar

- Workshop has a written purpose statement agreed with sponsor before invitations go out
- Every working block has an explicit output (not "discussion")
- Every decision has owner + date — no "we agreed to think about it"
- Post-workshop output is shareable within 24 hours

## When NOT to use a workshop

- When the question is one-on-one (use an interview instead)
- When the decision is already made (don't theatre-perform consensus)
- When the right people can't attend in person/synchronously
