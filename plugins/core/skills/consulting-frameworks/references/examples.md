|---------------|----------------|

| **OPPORTUNITIES** | **THREATS** |
|-------------------|-------------|

2. **W+O:** Partner with fintechs to accelerate digital capabilities
3. **S+T:** Use relationship managers to defend against impersonal fintech competition
4. **W+T:** Prioritize core modernization to avoid disruption

## 5 Whys Example

### Problem: Customer complaints increased 40% this quarter

**Why 1:** Why did complaints increase?
> Response times to customer inquiries doubled from 24 to 48 hours.

**Why 2:** Why did response times double?
> Support team is handling 60% more tickets with same headcount.

**Why 3:** Why are there 60% more tickets?
> New product launch generated unexpected support volume.

**Why 4:** Why was volume unexpected?
> No support capacity planning was done for the launch.

**Why 5:** Why wasn't capacity planning done?
> Product and support teams don't have a joint launch readiness process.

**Root Cause:** Lack of cross-functional launch readiness process between Product and Support.

**Solution:** Implement launch readiness checklist requiring support capacity sign-off.

## Porter's Five Forces Example

### Scenario: Cloud ERP Market Analysis

**1. Threat of New Entrants: MEDIUM**
- High capital requirements for R&D
- Strong network effects favor incumbents
- BUT: Low-code platforms lowering barriers
- Rating: 3/5

**2. Supplier Power: LOW**
- Commoditized cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Multiple database options available
- Rating: 2/5

**3. Buyer Power: MEDIUM-HIGH**
- Large enterprises have negotiating leverage
- Switching costs are high but not prohibitive
- Growing SaaS options increase buyer choices
- Rating: 4/5

**4. Threat of Substitutes: MEDIUM**
- Best-of-breed point solutions
- Low-code/no-code alternatives
- Industry-specific solutions
- Rating: 3/5

**5. Competitive Rivalry: HIGH**
- SAP, Oracle, Microsoft, Workday competing aggressively
- Feature parity increasing
- Price competition intensifying
- Rating: 5/5

**Overall Industry Attractiveness:** Moderate (challenging competitive dynamics but large market)

## Recommendation Format Example

### Scenario: Technology Vendor Selection

```
RECOMMENDATION: Select Vendor A (CloudTech Solutions) for the CRM implementation

RATIONALE
Vendor A scored highest across all evaluation criteria, demonstrating 
superior product-market fit for our industry vertical, strongest 
integration capabilities with existing systems, and most competitive 
total cost of ownership over 5 years.

IMPACT
• Revenue enablement: 15% improvement in sales productivity (Year 1)
• Cost reduction: $2.3M saved vs. Vendor B over 5-year period
• Time to value: Go-live in 6 months vs. 9 months for alternatives
• Risk reduction: 95% customer retention rate, proven in our industry

REQUIREMENTS
• Investment: $1.8M implementation + $400K annual subscription
• Timeline: 6-month implementation, 2-month hypercare
• Dependencies: Data cleansing (must complete by Month 2)

RISKS & MITIGATIONS
• Integration complexity: Engage Vendor A's premium integration team
• Change adoption: Dedicated change management workstream
• Data migration: Phased approach with parallel running

NEXT STEPS
1. Contract negotiation - Procurement - Week 1-2
2. Kick-off planning - IT Lead - Week 2
3. Change management launch - HR - Week 3
4. Technical design sessions - Vendor A - Week 3-4
```
