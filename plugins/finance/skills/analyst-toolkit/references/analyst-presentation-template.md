# Analyst Presentation Template — Slide-by-Slide Skeleton

A reusable 12-slide skeleton for Omega financial deliverables. Adapt section depth to engagement scope.

## Slide 1 — Cover

```
[Omega Logo]                                   [Client Logo]

CLIENT NAME
PROJECT TITLE
Financial Analysis & Recommendations

Version X.Y       DD MMM YYYY
Prepared by [Author Name]
Reviewed by [Reviewer Name]
Omega Consulting
```

**Speaker note:** Open with client name and one-sentence project description.

---

## Slide 2 — Executive Summary

**Title (headline-style):** "[Client] is bankable / faces 3 key risks / requires X to proceed"

**Body:** 4-5 bullets, each a finding + the supporting number.

Example:
- Revenue grew 14 % CAGR but EBITDA declined 8 % — margin compression of 530 bps
- Working capital intensity rose from 15 % to 22 % of sales — EGP 84 M cash tied up
- Net leverage at 2.8x EBITDA — within covenant but limits new debt capacity
- Sensitivity shows project IRR > WACC in 8 of 10 stress cases
- **Recommendation: Conditional approval, subject to working capital plan**

**Speaker note:** This is the only slide most executives will read. Make it self-sufficient.

---

## Slide 3 — Engagement Context

**Title:** "Why we are here, what we were asked to do"

**Body:** A 3-element diagram:
1. **The situation** — what triggered the engagement
2. **The complication** — what's at stake
3. **The question** — what we were asked

**Speaker note:** Confirms alignment with the client's brief in their own language.

---

## Slide 4 — Approach

**Title:** "Our 5-step analytical approach, completed in [X] weeks"

**Body:** Horizontal arrow diagram with 5 steps:
```
1. Data collection → 2. Statement analysis → 3. Ratio benchmarking → 
4. Scenario modeling → 5. Recommendation synthesis
```

**Speaker note:** Notes 1 line under each step on what was done.

---

## Slide 5 — Historical Financial Performance

**Title:** "Revenue up 20 %, EBITDA down 8 % — quality issue"

**Body:**
- Left: 3-line indexed trend chart (Revenue, EBITDA, Net Income) over 3-5 years, indexed to 100
- Right: 4-row table with key metrics and YoY changes

**Speaker note:** Walk through the divergence between top line and bottom line.

---

## Slide 6 — Common-Size P&L

**Title:** "Gross margin compressed 4.5 pts; operating leverage thinning"

**Body:** Single common-size table showing FY{t-2}, FY{t-1}, FY{t} as % of revenue. Color the changes: red for deterioration, green for improvement.

**Speaker note:** Frame whether the compression is volume-driven or cost-driven.

---

## Slide 7 — Ratio Dashboard

**Title:** "Liquidity strong, leverage moderate, profitability weakening"

**Body:** 4-quadrant ratio dashboard — Liquidity / Solvency / Profitability / Growth — each quadrant with 3-4 ratios, color-coded vs sector median.

**Speaker note:** Lead with the strongest and weakest dimensions; don't list all 16 ratios verbally.

---

## Slide 8 — Working Capital Analysis

**Title:** "EGP 84 M tied in working capital growth; AR collection is the lever"

**Body:**
- DSO / DIO / DPO / CCC chart over time
- Quantified cash release for each 10-day improvement

**Speaker note:** This is often where the largest near-term cash opportunity lives.

---

## Slide 9 — Sensitivity / Scenarios

**Title:** "Project IRR remains > WACC in 8 of 10 stress scenarios"

**Body:** Tornado chart (single most impactful slide). Top 6 sensitivities, ranked, with the range of outcomes.

**Speaker note:** Identify the top 3 contract-negotiation levers (the dominant tornado bars).

---

## Slide 10 — Risks and Mitigations

**Title:** "Three material risks; each has a defined mitigation pathway"

**Body:** Three-column table:

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| FX volatility | High | High | Natural hedging via USD revenue lock-in |
| Receivable concentration | Medium | High | Diversify customer base; credit insurance |
| Input cost inflation | High | Medium | Pass-through clause in 60 % of contracts |

**Speaker note:** Connect each risk to a specific business unit / contract owner.

---

## Slide 11 — Recommendations

**Title:** "Five actions, sequenced over 90 days, owners identified"

**Body:** Numbered list with the four columns:

| # | Action | Owner | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tighten DSO from 60 → 45 days | CFO | 30 days |
| 2 | Renegotiate top 3 supply contracts | COO | 60 days |
| 3 | Refinance short-term to long-term debt | CFO + Treasurer | 90 days |
| 4 | Implement monthly cash flow forecasting | FP&A | 30 days |
| 5 | Formal credit policy approval | Board | 90 days |

**Speaker note:** Each action has a measurable success metric.

---

## Slide 12 — Next Steps

**Title:** "Decision required on [X] by [date]; Omega support available through Q[N]"

**Body:** Timeline with the decision gates and Omega deliverables aligned underneath.

**Speaker note:** Close with a single specific ask.

---

## Optional appendix slides

- A1: Detailed three-statement summary (3 years actual + 3 years projected)
- A2: Full ratio table (all 24 ratios)
- A3: Loan amortization schedule (if relevant)
- A4: Methodology and data sources
- A5: Glossary
- A6: Team and contacts

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every slide title is a complete sentence with a message
- [ ] Every slide has 1 chart OR 1 table (not both unless essential)
- [ ] Slide 2 (Exec Summary) is self-sufficient
- [ ] Color palette is Omega-only
- [ ] No pie charts anywhere
- [ ] Speaker notes are filled in for slides 2-11
- [ ] Page numbers and footer (client / date / "Confidential")
- [ ] File name follows Omega convention
