## Egypt/Suez Region Specifics

### Regulatory Framework
- SCZONE (Suez Canal Economic Zone) Authority
- General Authority for Investment (GAFI)
- Egyptian Port Authority oversight

### Currency Considerations
- Revenue typically USD-denominated
- Operating costs in EGP
- Exchange rate risk on cost side
- Consider hedging mechanisms

### Tax Regime
- Corporate tax: 22.5%
- SCZONE incentives available
- Withholding on dividends: 5-10%
- Double taxation treaties

### Political Risk Mitigation
- MIGA coverage available
- Bilateral investment treaties
- International arbitration (ICSID)
- Government guarantee mechanisms
