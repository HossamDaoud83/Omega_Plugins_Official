# IMO Conventions Quick Reference

## SOLAS (Safety of Life at Sea)

### Key Chapters
| Chapter | Focus |
|---------|-------|
| II-1 | Construction - subdivision, stability, machinery |
| II-2 | Fire protection, detection, extinction |
| III | Life-saving appliances |
| IV | Radio communications |
| V | Safety of navigation |
| VI | Carriage of cargoes |
| VII | Dangerous goods |
| IX | ISM Code (management for safe operation) |
| XI-1 | Special measures to enhance safety |
| XI-2 | ISPS Code (maritime security) |

### SOLAS Certificates
- Safety Construction Certificate
- Safety Equipment Certificate
- Safety Radio Certificate
- Cargo Ship Safety Certificate

---

## MARPOL (Marine Pollution)

### Annexes
| Annex | Subject | Entry into Force |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| I | Oil pollution | 1983 |
| II | Noxious liquid substances | 1987 |
| III | Harmful substances in packaged form | 1992 |
| IV | Sewage | 2003 |
| V | Garbage | 1988 |
| VI | Air pollution | 2005 |

### MARPOL Annex VI - Air Pollution
- **IMO 2020:** Global sulfur cap 0.5%
- **ECA Zones:** 0.1% sulfur limit
- **EEXI:** Energy Efficiency Existing Ship Index
- **CII:** Carbon Intensity Indicator
- **SEEMP:** Ship Energy Efficiency Management Plan

---

## ISM Code (International Safety Management)

### Requirements
1. Safety and environmental protection policy
2. Company responsibilities and authority
3. Designated Person Ashore (DPA)
4. Master's responsibility and authority
5. Resources and personnel
6. Shipboard operations plans
7. Emergency preparedness
8. Non-conformity, accidents, incidents
9. Maintenance of ship and equipment
10. Documentation
11. Company verification, review, evaluation
12. Certification and verification

### Certificates
- Document of Compliance (DOC) - company
- Safety Management Certificate (SMC) - ship

---

## ISPS Code (International Ship and Port Facility Security)

### Security Levels
| Level | Condition |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Normal - minimum security measures |
| 2 | Heightened - additional measures |
| 3 | Exceptional - further specific measures |

### Requirements
- Ship Security Plan (SSP)
- Port Facility Security Plan (PFSP)
- Ship Security Officer (SSO)
- Port Facility Security Officer (PFSO)
- Company Security Officer (CSO)
- International Ship Security Certificate (ISSC)

---

## MLC 2006 (Maritime Labour Convention)

### Titles
1. Minimum requirements for seafarers
2. Conditions of employment
3. Accommodation, recreation, food, catering
4. Health protection, medical care, welfare, social security
5. Compliance and enforcement

### Certificates
- Maritime Labour Certificate
- Declaration of Maritime Labour Compliance

---

## Ballast Water Management Convention

### Requirements
- Ballast Water Management Plan
- Ballast Water Record Book
- D-1 Standard (exchange)
- D-2 Standard (treatment)
- International Ballast Water Management Certificate
