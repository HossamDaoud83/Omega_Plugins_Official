## Regulatory Framework

### International Maritime Organization (IMO)
- **SOLAS:** Safety of Life at Sea
- **MARPOL:** Marine pollution prevention
- **ISM Code:** International Safety Management
- **ISPS Code:** Ship and port security
- **MLC 2006:** Maritime Labour Convention

### Environmental Regulations
- **IMO 2020:** Sulfur emissions cap (0.5%)
- **EEXI/CII:** Energy efficiency requirements
- **Ballast Water Management:** BWM Convention
- **MARPOL Annex VI:** Air pollution prevention

### Security & Trade
- **ISPS Code:** Security requirements
- **C-TPAT:** Customs-Trade Partnership
- **AEO:** Authorized Economic Operator
- **24-Hour Rule:** Advance cargo declarations
